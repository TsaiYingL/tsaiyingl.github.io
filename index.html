More About Me...

I am a junior at the Georgia Institute of Technology, having transferred from SUNY Stony Brook where I began my studies in computer science and joined the WISE Honors program. While Stony Brook gave me a strong foundation in software and theory, I discovered that I was equally drawn to working with hardware, which motivated my move to Georgia Tech

At my new school, I joined the VIP program called Neckton, where I focused on the software side of marine robotics. Through this experience, I gained exposure to ROS2, collaborative coding practices, and the challenges of building reliable systems for underwater environments

In the mean time, I became involved with Georgia Tech’s Marine Robotics Group, where my role leaned more toward electrical engineering. I learned practical skills such as soldering, wiring, and integrating sensors with embedded systems. This hands‑on work gave me a deeper appreciation for how hardware and software must come together seamlessly in robotics

Moving forward, I look foward to collaborate with like-minded people and eager to contribute my skills and experiences to innovative projects through both research and internships.
